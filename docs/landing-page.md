# Landing & Waitlist Page: Design Brief

## Goal
Collect waitlist sign-ups from people learning Korean or Japanese.
Success = % of visitors who join the waitlist.

## Audience
Anyone who wants to *actually talk* in Korean or Japanese, not just pass
tests:
- Learners from home: K-drama, K-pop, anime and culture fans
- People planning to travel, study, work or move there
- People with Korean or Japanese friends, partners or family
- Beginners welcome
Most visitors arrive from Instagram on their phones → design mobile-first.

## Core message
Most apps correct your grammar. None teach you *how to talk to who*.
Kotoba lets you practice real conversations in Korean and Japanese with
characters who remember how you treated them, and react when you get
politeness wrong.

## Page sections (top to bottom)
1. **Hero:** Kotoba, one-line promise, waitlist button. Later: a 15–20 s
   demo clip (a 반말 slip and the character's reaction).
2. **The problem:** "반말 or 존댓말? です/ます or casual? You've learned
   the words, but how do you talk to a coworker, a friend's parent, a
   neighbor?"
3. **How it works (3 steps):** Talk out loud with a character → they react
   to how you speak → the relationship changes over time.
4. **Meet the characters:** Seo-yeon (your 선배 at a Seoul startup) and Ren
   (your neighbor in Japan). Portrait + one line each.
5. **Who it's for:** anyone learning Korean or Japanese who wants to speak
   like a real person, from total beginners to people heading there.
6. **Waitlist form** (see below)
7. **FAQ:** When is it out? (iPhone beta, spring 2027) · Is it for
   beginners? (yes) · Which languages? (Korean and Japanese) · Do I need to
   be moving there? (no)
8. **Footer:** contact, Instagram/X links, privacy note

## Waitlist form fields
- Email (required)
- Language: Korean / Japanese / Both
- Level: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced
- Why are you learning? Culture (K-drama, K-pop, anime…) / Travel /
  Moving or living there / Friends, partner or family / Work or school /
  Just for fun
- Checkbox: "I'm 18 or older" (required)

## Assets needed from design
- Logo / wordmark, color palette, fonts
- Character portraits for Seo-yeon and Ren (a few expressions if possible)
- Social preview image (what shows when the link is shared)
